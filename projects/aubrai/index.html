AUBRAI is the first BioAgent launched on Bio Protocol's launchpad. It went live on Base on 26 August 2025 after an Ignition Sale that closed 14.62x oversubscribed. The agent is co-developed by VitaDAO and BIO Protocol with scientific direction from Aubrey de Grey's LEV Foundation. It runs on Phala Cloud TEE inside ElizaOS v2, with a knowledge graph built on 4,000+ curated longevity papers, a 10k-pair synthetic Q&A fine-tune, and unpublished lab notes from de Grey's research.

Hypotheses minted by AUBRAI are hashed on Base via Molecule's Proof-of-Invention infrastructure. Inference currently runs on Claude Opus 4.6 according to the agent's live terminal at aubr.ai. The flagship use case for AUBRAI's revenue is funding LEVF's Robust Mouse Rejuvenation 2 (RMR2) program, an ambitious study designed to double the remaining lifespan of middle-aged mice. AUBRAI directs trading-fee revenue and Bio treasury grants from the BIOPSY-30 proposal toward this work. As of May 2026 the archive contains 5,930 hypothesis evaluations spanning topics from senolytics to mitochondrial allotopic expression.

// 07 RMR2 FLAGSHIP ROBUST MOUSE REJUVENATION STUDY 2 · MID-2026 · RESULTS 2028

RMR2 is LEVF's second-generation Robust Mouse Rejuvenation study. RMR1, completed in 2023, tested seven single-intervention arms. RMR2 expands to 20 treatment combinations across 8 named interventions, with ~50 mice per group and up to 2,000 mice total in C57BL/6J or HET3 backgrounds. All animals except true controls receive rapamycin baseline plus running-wheel exercise. Interventions begin at 18 to 20 months of age. The combinatorial design is what makes RMR2 ambitious: previous longevity work tested single agents and found incremental gains. RMR2 tests whether stacking complementary interventions produces multiplicative, not just additive, lifespan extension.

// 8 INTERVENTIONS
  1. 01D-PUFAs, deuterated polyunsaturated fatty acids, inhibiting lipid peroxidation
  2. 02Recombinant mouse serum albumin
  3. 03Mesenchymal stem cells, replacing RMR1's hematopoietic stem cells
  4. 04Partial cellular reprogramming via Yamanaka factors (Oct4 / Sox2 / Klf4 / c-Myc)
  5. 05IL-11 inhibition
  6. 06Cdc42 inhibition with CASIN
  7. 07LC-FACS-based selective senolysis
  8. 08Oxytocin therapy
