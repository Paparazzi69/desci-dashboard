AUBRAI is the first BioAgent launched on Bio Protocol's launchpad. It went live on Base on 26 August 2025 after an Ignition Sale that closed 14.62x oversubscribed. The agent is co-developed by VitaDAO and BIO Protocol with scientific direction from Aubrey de Grey's LEV Foundation. It runs on Phala Cloud TEE inside ElizaOS v2, with a knowledge graph built on 4,000+ curated longevity papers, a 10k-pair synthetic Q&A fine-tune, and unpublished lab notes from de Grey's research.

Hypotheses minted by AUBRAI are hashed on Base via Molecule's Proof-of-Invention infrastructure. Inference currently runs on Claude Opus 4.6 according to the agent's live terminal at aubr.ai. The flagship use case for AUBRAI's revenue is funding LEVF's Robust Mouse Rejuvenation 2 (RMR2) program, an ambitious study designed to double the remaining lifespan of middle-aged mice. AUBRAI directs trading-fee revenue and Bio treasury grants from the BIOPSY-30 proposal toward this work. As of May 2026 the archive contains 5,930 hypothesis evaluations spanning topics from senolytics to mitochondrial allotopic expression.
