AUBRAI is the first BioAgent launched on Bio Protocol's launchpad. It went live on Base on 26 August 2025 after an Ignition Sale that closed 14.62x oversubscribed. The agent is co-developed by VitaDAO and BIO Protocol with scientific direction from Aubrey de Grey's LEV Foundation. It runs on Phala Cloud TEE inside ElizaOS v2, with a knowledge graph built on 4,000+ curated longevity papers, a 10k-pair synthetic Q&A fine-tune, and unpublished lab notes from de Grey's research.

Hypotheses minted by AUBRAI are hashed on Base via Molecule's Proof-of-Invention infrastructure. Inference currently runs on Claude Opus 4.6 according to the agent's live terminal at aubr.ai. The flagship use case for AUBRAI's revenue is funding LEVF's Robust Mouse Rejuvenation 2 (RMR2) program, an ambitious study designed to double the remaining lifespan of middle-aged mice. AUBRAI directs trading-fee revenue and Bio treasury grants from the BIOPSY-30 proposal toward this work. As of May 2026 the archive contains 5,930 hypothesis evaluations spanning topics from senolytics to mitochondrial allotopic expression.

// 04 HYPOTHESIS-TO-FUNDING FLYWHEEL PROMPT TO POI TO GRANT TO RESULT, REPEATED
// 01
PROMPT
User asks AUBRAI a longevity-research question on chat.aubr.ai or via the X-thread workflow.
// 02
HYPOTHESIS
AUBRAI generates and refines candidate hypotheses with cited literature. Returns ranked compounds, mechanisms, safety data, trial readiness when prompted in deep-research mode.
// 03
POI MINT
Top hypotheses are timestamped on Base as Proof-of-Invention NFTs through Molecule's infrastructure. The on-chain record creates a verifiable claim of authorship.
// 04
FUND
Bio treasury and ResearchHub deploy capital to the strongest hypotheses. The BIOPSY-30 proposal committed 100,000 $RSC for this purpose. AUBRAI's own trading-fee revenue routes to LEVF for RMR2.
// 05
FEEDBACK
Results, validations, and failures feed back into AUBRAI's knowledge graph for the next iteration.

This is the architecture AUBRAI's marketing calls agentic science. In practice it is a feedback loop where prompts produce hypotheses, hypotheses produce on-chain claims, on-chain claims produce funding decisions, and funded work produces validation that improves the next prompt response. The loop is the product.
