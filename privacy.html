Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: May 5, 2026

DeSci Analytics Hub is a curated, read-only tracker for the Decentralized Science sector. The site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and stores no user data. This page documents what data does pass through and which third parties may see it.

What the site does

DeSci Analytics Hub is a static dashboard. It displays token prices, BioAgent and IPT pipeline data, and curated project deep-dives. There are no user accounts, no logins, and no forms. Reading the site does not generate user records on our end.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare automatically logs standard HTTP request metadata: IP address, user-agent string, request URL, and timestamp. Cloudflare uses these logs for caching, DDoS protection, and abuse handling. We do not run a separate analytics layer on top of these logs and do not export them anywhere.

Third parties that may see your request

  • Cloudflare (hosting, CDN, security): receives all request metadata as described above.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): the site loads two font families (Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono) from Google's CDN. Your browser makes those requests directly to Google, which can see your IP address and user-agent. Google states it does not use Google Fonts requests to build advertising profiles.
  • CoinGecko (token price API), called from your browser when you load the dashboard. CoinGecko sees your IP and user-agent for those API requests.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Cloudflare may set a functional security cookie (cf_clearance, _cfuvid, or similar) when its WAF challenges a request. These cookies are operational, not for analytics, and are managed by Cloudflare, not by us.

No analytics, no tracking

There are no analytics scripts on this site. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no Mixpanel, no PostHog. There are no tracking pixels, no advertising tags, and no behavioral profiling code. Verified at time of writing by inspecting all HTML and JS in the repository.

Contact

For data requests, corrections, or removal questions, reach out via Twitter: @DeSciDashboard.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last reviewed" date at the top of this page.
