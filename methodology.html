Agent status definitions

Live

Agent or IPT is operational on mainnet, has produced verifiable output (hypotheses, IP-NFTs, programme progress, clinical milestones, or confirmed market activity), and has communicated within the last 90 days.

Launching

Agent is in pre-mainnet phase. Token sale, ignition phase, or pre-clinical activity confirmed. Programme structure announced, but main token or product not yet live.

Announced

Confirmed in roadmap or announcement by a credible source (parent DAO, founder, Bio Protocol). No on-chain activity yet. Form factor (token, IPT, infrastructure) may still be undefined.

Stalled

Was active but has frozen progress. No new launches, no significant on-chain activity for 60+ days, or explicit pause statement from operators. Tracked separately so the audience can see honest sector state, not just the highlights.

What we don't track

The tracker is curated, not a directory. The following categories are deliberately excluded:

  • Memecoins without scientific output. Tokens that use DeSci aesthetics but produce no verifiable research, hypotheses, or programme progress. Pump.science compounds are tracked because they tie to actual mouse experiments. Most "DeSci memes" do not.
  • Closed or private projects. No public agent dashboard, no on-chain activity, no DAO communication. If we can't verify it, we don't list it.
  • Generic AI agents. Cookie.fun, Virtuals, ai16z, and similar ecosystems track thousands of AI agents. We only include those with explicit biology, science, or research programmes.
  • Trading bots branded as "BioAgents." Any agent whose primary function is portfolio management or trading, regardless of its name.
  • Tokenized supplements without programme. A token tied to a supplement but with no clinical trial, no research output, and no DAO governance.
  • Projects with credible fraud signals. Confirmed exit scams, founder-token rugs without resolution, or active legal action against operators.

If a project belongs to one of these categories but you believe we should include it, see "Suggest an agent" below.

Editorial criteria

For an agent or IPT to qualify for the tracker, it must meet at least one item in each of three dimensions:

Verifiability

  • On-chain activity (token contract, IP-NFT, sale, or treasury transactions), or
  • Public programme progress (gates passed, hypotheses minted, papers published), or
  • Confirmed clinical or wet-lab milestone.

Credibility

  • Operated by a recognized DAO, protocol, or research lab, or
  • Founder publicly identified with verifiable credentials, or
  • Backed by a known DeSci VC, accelerator, or institutional partner.

DeSci scope

  • Targets biology, longevity, drug discovery, neuroscience, or related research domain, or
  • Provides infrastructure for DeSci agents (e.g. BIOS Data Agent, ClawdLab), or
  • Generates IP, datasets, or compounds that can be tokenized or commercialized.

A project that fails any dimension is not included, even if popular on social media.

Chain coverage

The tracker uses chain badges on every card to show where each agent or IPT is deployed.

  • Base
    Coinbase L2. Most Bio Protocol BioAgents (PeptAI, AUBRAI, BiomeAI, Holi, others) and IPTs (VITA-FAST, CLAW, VitaSTEM, VitaRNA, HOLI) live here.
  • Solana
    Primary chain for DeSci memecoin liquidity (pump.science, Curetopia / CURES) and AI-agent-native experiments (Spore.fun adjacent ecosystem).
  • Multi
    Agent operates across multiple chains or is chain-agnostic infrastructure (BIOS Data Agent, ClawdLab).
  • Ethereum
    Older deployments and some legacy IP-NFT structures (DermaLabs).
  • TBD
    Chain not yet announced. Typically applies to ANNOUNCED status agents.

Coverage is independent of chain. The tracker treats each ecosystem on its own merit.

Last signal

Every agent card shows a "Last Signal" field with a date and short description. This is the most recent verified action by the agent or its operators, drawn from one of:

  • Official tweets from the parent organization (Bio Protocol, VitaDAO, CerebrumDAO, Molecule, others).
  • bio.xyz launchpad or agent dashboard updates.
  • molecule.xyz IPT page changes.
  • Published research outputs (arXiv, Molecule Labs, GitHub releases).
  • Confirmed on-chain events (IP-NFT mints, sale completions, treasury approvals).

A signal counts only if it represents progress against the agent's stated programme. Marketing tweets, generic engagement posts, and recycled announcements are excluded.

Last Signal is reviewed weekly. If an agent has had no qualifying signal for 60+ days, we evaluate moving it to STALLED.

Independence

The DeSci Dashboard BioAgent Tracker is independently operated. It is not affiliated with, paid by, or endorsed by Bio Protocol, Molecule, VitaDAO, CerebrumDAO, ResearchHub, pump.science, or any other DeSci entity covered on the page.

Coverage is on merit. Solana-native projects are not preferred or penalized relative to Base-native projects. STALLED status is applied based on observable signal, not relationship. We retain editorial discretion on which agents to include in the tracker, with criteria documented above.

We do not accept payment for inclusion, ranking, or commentary. If this changes, it will be disclosed at the top of the page. Disagree with a placement? Reach us on X at @DeSciDashboard.

Conflict of interest

The author may hold small positions in tokens listed on the tracker, including but not limited to $BIO, $VITA, $RSC, and BioAgent tokens covered on this page. Position size is not material to portfolio (under 5% per token). No position exceeds the ability to be sold without market impact.

Holdings are disclosed in good faith. If a position becomes material or if the author takes a paid advisory role with a tracked entity, this section will be updated within 24 hours.
