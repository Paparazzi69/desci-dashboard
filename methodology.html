Methodology & Data Sources

Updated weekly

Every number on this site can be traced back to a source. This page documents how we collect, verify, and update token prices, BioAgent pipeline stages, and signal feeds — so you can decide what to trust.

Pipeline stage definitions

A BioAgent's pipeline stage answers one question: where is its most-advanced programme? Not its average — its frontier.

StageWhat qualifiesExample
01 · Hypothesis AI generates scientific hypotheses, mints them onchain via bio.xyz. AUBRAI minting 1,000+ hypotheses
02 · Computational In-silico validation, peptide design, binding prediction, structural docking. PeptAI's 8-gate validation pipeline
03 · Wet Lab Synthesis, binding assays, cell-level testing, compound validation. OX2R-004 advancing to wet-lab validation
04 · Clinical Animal models, Phase 1/2 trials, FDA / EMA pathway. VITA-FAST progressing toward Phase 2

Agent status definitions

Live

Agent is deployed on mainnet, actively producing verifiable output — hypotheses, compound candidates, papers, or IP-NFTs.

Launching

Agent is on the bio.xyz launchpad. Token sale completed or in progress, pre-mainnet, programmes in design.

Announced

Confirmed in the Bio Protocol roadmap or by a BioDAO, but no launchpad listing yet. Ghost cards on the tracker.

Independence statement

This tracker is independent and not affiliated with Bio Protocol or any BioDAO. Numbers are indicative; pipeline stage is a weekly judgement call based on publicly available information. Disagree with a placement? Open an issue.
