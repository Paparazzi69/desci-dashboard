// 02 RECEPTOR PRIMER BIOLOGY-FIRST · OX2R 101

What OX2R does.

// THE RECEPTOR-LEVEL EXPLAINER. SKIP IF YOU ALREADY KNOW.

// MECHANISM · SIX LINES
  • OX2R = orexin receptor type 2.
  • Coupled to Gq protein, also Gi/Gs in some tissues.
  • Expressed in hypothalamus, locus coeruleus, dorsal raphe.
  • Activation drives wakefulness, arousal, attention.
  • Drives histaminergic arousal in the tuberomammillary nucleus.
  • Loss-of-function in mice produces a narcolepsy phenotype.
// THERAPEUTIC TARGETS · FOUR LINES
  • Narcolepsy type 1, where orexin neurons are destroyed. Clinical-stage OX2R agonists (TAK-861, ORX750, BP1.15205) target this as small molecules.
  • ADHD subtypes with measurably low orexin tone.
  • Idiopathic hypersomnia.
  • Treatment-resistant fatigue and arousal disorders.
// CAVEAT · ALL HYPOTHETICAL. NO APPROVED OX2R AGONIST EXISTS TODAY. OX2R-004 HAS NOT BEEN TESTED IN ANY ANIMAL OR HUMAN. RECEPTOR SELECTIVITY OVER OX1R IS COMPUTATIONAL ONLY.

R-01 · NO BIOLOGICAL DATA YET
OX2R-004 has not yet been synthesised. Phase 1 wet-lab plan published on Molecule mainnet with $7,750 budget split across Adaptyv Bio (BLI binding affinity plus OX1R counter-screen), Ginkgo Bioworks (cAMP HTRF functional assay on top binders), and plasma-stability LC-MS. 4-6 week turnaround expected from synthesis to first data.
R-02 · GATE THRESHOLDS NOT PRE-REGISTERED
Pass/fail criteria for each gate are visible per-decision but not published as a single specification. The bar for what counts as 'passing' could move between candidates.
R-03 · PRE-IND IS NOT REGULATORY
The pre-IND document is a marketing-and-design artifact, not an FDA filing. OX2R-004 is years and multiple Phase trials away from any patient.
R-04 · TARGET VALIDATION INCOMPLETE
Existing clinical-stage OX2R agonists (TAK-861, ORX750, BP1.15205) target narcolepsy as small molecules. The ADHD orexin hypothesis is supported by population studies (drug-naive children show decreased orexin-A/B levels and evening chronotype dominance) but not by interventional trials. Even if OX2R-004 binds perfectly, the disease model itself remains a hypothesis.
