Peer-Reviewed Research

Live academic record for DeSci projects, curated from Europe PMC and filtered to Medline (peer-reviewed only, no preprints or books). Each project's query is hand-built and verified so only papers genuinely tied to it surface. Two kinds of signal: papers by a project's named scientists (team track record), and papers that name a project in their funding or acknowledgements. Sorted by citations, every title links to a working DOI. The list grows as more projects gain a verified query.
